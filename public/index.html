Frequently Asked Questions

What is full surrender and how fast do I get the money?
Full surrender terminates the policy and pays you the full cash surrender value. Most families receive funds in 3-7 business days directly to their bank account. It shows clearly in your dashboard.
Can I cancel anytime and take the money for emergencies?
Yes. This is exactly what the emergency cash feature is designed for. You can do a full or partial surrender directly from your profile at any time.
Does my family still stay protected after I withdraw money?
Yes. Even after withdrawals, the remaining coverage continues to protect your family. You can also reactivate or increase coverage later.
Is this available in Germany / Europe?
Yes. We support German residents with full German language support and local banking options. Many families in Berlin, Munich, and Dortmund are already using it.
