Frequently Asked Questions

What happens with full surrender for emergency cash?
The policy terminates and you receive the cash surrender value. It is clearly recorded on your profile in the Transaction History as an "Emergency Cash Out" with the exact amount and date.
Is the emergency cash out visible on my profile?
Yes. After full surrender or withdrawals, your dashboard shows the policy status and a detailed Transaction History listing every cash out event.
